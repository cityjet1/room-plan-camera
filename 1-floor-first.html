Floor first.
Then the corners.

Experimental measurement method. Not validated on your phone. Do not use these measurements for EPC work or purchasing materials.
  1. Find a clear patch of the actual floor. Move the phone slowly, aiming down.
  2. When a possible floor location appears, confirm the circle is on the actual floor. The browser can report an upward direction without measuring the surface orientation.
  3. Aim the centre circle at each wall-floor corner. Add the corners in order, then close the room.

This experiment uses a mapped floor and the phone's tracked position. It does not use depth readings. No app install, account sign-in or Google account connection is requested. Browser AR still depends on the phone's existing AR support.

If tracking is lost or the page is interrupted, the unfinished room is discarded. This prototype does not claim automatic tracking recovery.

One room only. Height, doors, windows, compass and export are deliberately outside this first experiment. Nothing is uploaded or saved by this page.
